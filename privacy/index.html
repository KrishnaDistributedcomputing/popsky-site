Pop Sky: Balloon Pop Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Data and processing

Pop Sky: Balloon Pop (“Pop Sky”) is a casual game. Progress, coins, shop unlocks, and a short widget snapshot are stored on your device in an App Group shared by the app and its widget.

The app does not create an account, does not show ads, and does not include a tracking or analytics SDK. It does not send your game progress to a developer server.

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, Apple may receive scores and achievement progress through Game Center. If you choose to play with SharePlay, Apple’s FaceTime and SharePlay systems carry the session. Those services are provided by Apple, not by a developer-operated server.

Optional permissions

Camera

On supported devices, the camera is used so balloons can appear in the room (AR), for the optional Camera Lens backdrop, and for the cheek-puff bonus on devices with a TrueDepth camera. Images are processed on the device for those features and are not saved or uploaded by the app. If you decline, the painted sky remains and AR features stay unavailable.

Microphone

During Blow moments, a puff of air can push balloons. The sound level is metered on the device and is not saved or uploaded. If you decline, the Gust button does the same push.

Motion

Tilting the device can steer the wind. If motion is unavailable (for example in Simulator), a wind slider appears instead.

Widgets

The Home Screen widget reads an on-device snapshot of your streak. It uses a shared container on the same device.

Purchases, exports and support

Purchases: Pop Sky is free and has no in-app purchases.

Exports: There is no export of an account, because there is no account.

Support: If you email for support, only the information you choose to include in your message is received. That email is used only to respond to your request.

Choices and deletion

You can revoke camera, microphone, or motion access in iOS Settings.

Uninstalling Pop Sky removes the app’s on-device data, including App Group storage used by the widget, unless a device backup you control still contains it.

Optional Game Center data remains under Apple’s Game Center controls.

Pop Sky is designed for any age. It does not show ads and does not ask children for personal information.
