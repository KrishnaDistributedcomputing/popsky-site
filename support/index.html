Pop Sky: Balloon Pop Support

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Thanks for playing Pop Sky: Balloon Pop, a casual balloon-popping game for iPhone and iPad.

Quick help

  • Wind in the Simulator: use the on-screen slider. On a device, tilt to steer the wind.
  • Blow moments: allow the microphone, or use the Gust button if you prefer not to.
  • Camera Lens or AR: if you decline the camera, the painted sky stays and those optional features remain off.
  • Widget and Live Activity: add the Pop Sky widget from the Home Screen after you have opened the app once. Live Activities can appear during a round on supported devices.
  • Game Center and SharePlay: both are optional. SharePlay needs a FaceTime call with a friend.
